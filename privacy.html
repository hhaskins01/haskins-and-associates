Privacy Notice & POPIA

Website privacy notice · Last updated 10 August 2026

This website is operated for Haskins and Associates within Old Mutual Personal Financial Advice. This notice explains how personal information submitted through this website is handled. It is designed to support compliance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA), but it does not replace Old Mutual's own privacy notices or applicable contractual/regulatory requirements.

1. Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, selected associate and the message you choose to send. We ask you not to submit ID numbers, banking credentials, passwords, PINs, policy numbers, medical information or other sensitive information through the public website form.

2. Why we use it

We use enquiry information to respond to your request, route the enquiry to the associate you selected, communicate with you about that enquiry and meet applicable legal or regulatory obligations where required.

3. Marketing

Submitting an enquiry does not by itself constitute consent to receive unrelated marketing communications. Where marketing consent is required, it should be obtained separately and lawfully, with an appropriate opt-out mechanism.

4. Service providers

The website is hosted on Netlify when deployed through the supplied Netlify package. Website form submissions may therefore be processed and stored by Netlify as a hosting/form service provider. Netlify's own terms and privacy documentation apply to its processing. Only the information necessary for the enquiry should be submitted.

5. Security

The website uses HTTPS when served by Netlify and does not intentionally deploy advertising trackers or non-essential analytics. No website can guarantee absolute security, so please do not send confidential credentials through a public form.

6. Retention

Website enquiry information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected and for applicable legal, regulatory, dispute-resolution and record-keeping requirements. The appropriate retention period may depend on the nature of the enquiry and the systems used by Old Mutual.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law and any lawful limitations, you may have rights to request access to personal information, correction of inaccurate information, objection to certain processing and other rights provided by POPIA.

8. Requests and questions

For website-related privacy requests or questions, contact Hamilton Patrick Haskins at hhaskins@oldmutual.com. Where your request concerns information held by an Old Mutual group company, the relevant Old Mutual privacy process and notice may also apply.

9. Old Mutual privacy information

Old Mutual publishes its own privacy policy and may have separate privacy notices for specific products and services. Those notices take precedence for the information they govern.
