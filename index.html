Financial education

Questions worth asking.

How much should I have saved for retirement?

There is no universal number. Your target depends on income, time horizon, spending needs, assets, inflation and the retirement income you expect.

Do I need life cover?

Consider who depends on your income, debt obligations, future education costs and the financial impact of your death or disability.

Should I review my financial plan?

Major changes such as marriage, children, career changes, retirement, a business change or a move can justify a review.

What should I consider when planning my estate?

Consider your will, beneficiaries, asset ownership, liquidity needs and the people who may depend on your estate.
